# Chapter 20

## Translation

The masses are as merry, as if celebrating a springtime ritual.  
I alone am tranquil and unmoved.  
Formless, like an infant who hasn’t yet learned to smile,  
Drifting, as if I have nowhere to return to.

The masses have a surplus of ambition, but I alone seem to be empty.  
I have the heart of a fool\!  
The masses are bright. I alone am dark.  
The masses are sharp. I alone am withdrawn.

The masses are full of purpose, but I seem stubbornly useless.  
I alone differ from the masses. I value nothing above nursing from the great mother.
