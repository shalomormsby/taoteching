# Chapter 68

## Translation

*(untranslated)*

## Source

### Block 1

| 原文 | Pinyin | Literal |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 善為士者，不武； | Shàn wéi shì zhě, bù wǔ; | A masterful warrior/scholar is not martial/violent; |
| 善戰者，不怒； | Shàn zhàn zhě, bù nù; | A masterful fighter does not become angry; |
| 善勝敵者，不與； | Shàn shèng dí zhě, bù yǔ; | A masterful conqueror of enemies does not engage/contend with them; |
| 善用人者，為之下。 | Shàn yòng rén zhě, wéi zhī xià. | A masterful user of people functions as their subordinate/lower. |

### Block 2

| 原文 | Pinyin | Literal |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 是謂不爭之德， | Shì wèi bù zhēng zhī dé, | This is called the Virtue/Power (Te) of not competing/contending, |
| 是謂用人之力， | Shì wèi yòng rén zhī lì, | This is called the ability/power to use people, |
| 是謂配天，古之極。 | Shì wèi pèi tiān, gǔ zhī jí. | This is called matching/pairing with Heaven, the ultimate of antiquity. |
