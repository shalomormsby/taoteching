# Chapter 9

## Translation

Filling a vessel, stop before spilling.
An over-sharpened blade’s edge cannot last.

The hall of gold and jade can’t be guarded.
Those who seek wealth and status with arrogance bring ruin upon themselves.

When the work is done, cease striving. This is the way of the cosmos.
