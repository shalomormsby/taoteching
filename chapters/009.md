# Chapter 9

## Translation

Filling a vessel, stop before spilling.  
An over-sharpened blade’s edge cannot last.

The hall of gold and jade can’t be guarded.  
Those who seek wealth and status with arrogance bring ruin upon themselves.

When the work is done, cease striving. This is the way of nature.

## Notes

**Owed: the chapter's spine is three things you cannot keep, in three different characters, and the English keeps one.** *(2026-09-08, `WORKLIST.md` T2-51.)* 持 (*chí* — to hold; 說文 握也) opens it at 持而盈之 and is **not rendered**; 保 (*bǎo* — to keep safe; 說文 養也) closes the couplet at 不可長保 and is **not rendered**; only 守 (*shǒu* — 說文 守官也) survives, as *guarded*. The verse also invents a **vessel** — 器 (*qì*) is not in this chapter — and flattens 不如 (*bù rú* — not as good as) to a comma. **A stanza rewrite, not a term swap.** It should travel with T2-1 (身退 → *"cease striving,"* where *striving* is 爭's word) and T1-20's unlogged 銳 variant.
