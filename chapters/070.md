## Source

### Block 1

| 原文 | Pinyin | Literal |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 吾言甚易知，甚易行。 | Wú yán shèn yì zhī, shèn yì xíng. | My words are very easy to understand, very easy to execute. |
| 天下莫能知，莫能行。 | Tiān xià mò néng zhī, mò néng xíng. | No one in the world can understand them, no one can execute them. |

### Block 2

| 原文 | Pinyin | Literal |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 言有宗，事有君。 | Yán yǒu zōng, shì yǒu jūn. | Words have an ancestor/root, affairs have a sovereign/master. |
| 夫唯無知，是以不我知。 | Fú wéi wú zhī, shì yǐ bù wǒ zhī. | Precisely because they lack understanding, therefore they do not understand me. |

### Block 3

| 原文 | Pinyin | Literal |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 知我者希，則我者貴。 | Zhī wǒ zhě xī, zé wǒ zhě guì. | Those who understand me are rare, those who follow/model me are valued. |
| 是以聖人被褐而懷玉。 | Shì yǐ shèng rén pī hè ér huái yù. | Therefore the Sage wears coarse clothes but carries jade in their bosom. |
