# Chapter 10

## Translation

Being embodied and embracing the One, can you remain whole?  
Flowing with life energy and achieving softness, can you be like a newborn?

Cleaning the mysterious mirror, can you see what truly is?  
Governing people with love, can you lead without effort?

As the gates of the cosmos open and close, can you embody the flow of the feminine?  
With an understanding that spans the four directions, can you be unknowing?

Birthing and nourishing all things,  
Giving birth without possessing,  
Acting without presuming,  
Leading without mastering –  
This is Profound Integrity.
