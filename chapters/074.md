# Chapter 74

## Source

### Block 1

| 原文 | Pinyin | Literal |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 民不畏死，奈何以死懼之？ | Mín bù wèi sǐ, nài hé yǐ sǐ jù zhī? | When the people do not fear death, why use death to threaten them? |
| 若使民常畏死，而為奇者，吾得執而殺之，孰敢？ | Ruò shǐ mín cháng wèi sǐ, ér wéi qí zhě, wú dé zhí ér shā zhī, shú敢? | If you make the people constantly fear death, and those who perform odd/illegal acts, I can seize and kill them—who would dare? |

### Block 2

| 原文 | Pinyin | Literal |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 常有司殺者殺。 | Cháng yǒu sī shā zhě shā. | There is always a Master of Killing who kills. |
| 夫代司殺者殺，是謂代大匠斫。 | Fú dài sī shā zhě shā, shì wèi dài dà jiàng zhuó. | Now to take the place of the Master of Killing to kill, is called taking the place of the Great Carpenter to hew. |
| 夫代大匠斫，希有不傷其手矣。 | Fú dài dà jiàng zhuó, xī yǒu bù shāng qí shǒu yǐ. | To take the place of the Great Carpenter to hew, rarely is there anyone who does not injure their own hand. |
