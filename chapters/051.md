# Chapter 51

## Translation

The Tao gives birth to all things, integrity rears them,  
matter forms them, circumstance completes them.  
Therefore all things, without exception, revere the Tao and value integrity.  
This reverence for the Tao, this valuing of integrity — no one commands it.  
It is always so, of itself.

Thus, the Tao births all things and integrity rears them,  
grows and raises them, settles and matures them, nourishes and protects them.  
Giving birth without possessing,  
acting without presuming,  
leading without mastering –  
this is called mysterious integrity.
