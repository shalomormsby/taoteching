## Source

### Block 1

| 原文 | Pinyin | Literal |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 勇於敢則殺， | Yǒng yú gǎn zé shā, | Brave in daring, then (one) gets killed; |
| 勇於不敢則活。 | Yǒng yú bù gǎn zé huó. | Brave in not daring, then (one) lives. |
| 此兩者，或利或害。 | Cǐ liǎng zhě, huò lì huò hài. | These two things, one is beneficial, one is harmful. |
| 天之所惡，孰知其故？ | Tiān zhī suǒ wù, shú zhī qí gù? | That which Heaven dislikes, who knows the reason/cause? |

### Block 2

| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 天之道，不爭而善勝， | Tiān zhī dào, bù zhēng ér shàn shèng, | The Tao of Heaven: does not contend yet masterfully conquers, |
| 不言而善應， | Bù yán ér shàn yìng, | Does not speak yet masterfully responds, |
| 不召而自來， | Bù zhào ér zì lái, | Does not summon yet it comes by itself, |
| 繟然而善謀。 | Chǎn rán ér shàn móu. | It is loose/spacious yet masterfully plans. |
