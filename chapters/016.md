# Chapter 16

## Translation

Attain emptiness.
Embrace stillness.
The ten-thousand things arise together,
Relying upon this, I observe their return.

Multitudes flourish,
Each returns energy to its root.
Returning to the root is called Stillness.
This is called returning to your original nature.
Returning to your original nature is called the eternal.
Knowing the ever-present is called clear-seeing.

Not knowing the eternal brings reckless action and disaster.

Knowing the eternal creates the capacity to embrace the totality.
Embracing all leads to equanimity.
Equanimity leads to sovereignty.
Sovereignty leads to the cosmos.
The cosmos leads to the Tao.
The Tao leads to the eternal.
Not even death threatens this.
