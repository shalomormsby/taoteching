# Chapter 38

## Translation

The highest integrity isn’t concerned with integrity, therefore it has integrity.  
Lesser integrity clutches at integrity, therefore it lacks integrity.  
The highest integrity takes no action and has no deliberate motive.  
Lesser integrity takes action and has a deliberate motive.

Ultimate benevolence takes action yet has no deliberate motive.  
Ultimate righteousness takes action and has a deliberate motive.  
Ultimate ritualism takes action, and when no one responds, it bares its arms and forces them.

Therefore, when the Tao is lost, there is pure integrity.  
When integrity is lost, there is benevolence.  
When benevolence is lost, there is righteousness.  
When righteousness is lost, there is ritualism.  
Ritualism is the waning of integrity and the beginning of chaos.  
Predicting the future is the flowery imitation of the Tao and the beginning of foolishness.

Therefore, the mature person dwells in the thickness of being, not in its thinness,  
Dwells in life’s fruit, not in flowery display,  
Rejects surfaces and embraces life’s thickness.
