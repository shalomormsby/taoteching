# Chapter 35

## Translation

Embody the Great Form, and the world routes to you.
This route is free of harm. It offers safety, peace, and stability.
The allure of music and fine food causes passing guests to stop.

Attempt to speak the Tao. It is bland and flavorless.
Look for it. It can’t be seen.
Listen for it. It can’t be heard.
Use it. It is inexhaustible.
