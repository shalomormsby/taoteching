# Chapter 49

## Translation

The Sage has no fixed heart-mind;
they take the heart-mind of the people as their own.

The good, I am good to;
the not good, I am also good to,
for integrity is good.

The trustworthy, I trust.
The untrustworthy, I also trust,
for integrity is trustworthy.

Living amidst others in the world, the Sage breathes in the world
and merges their heart-mind with the world.
The people all strain their ears and eyes;
the Sage nurtures them all like newborns.
