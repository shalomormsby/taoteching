# Chapter 11

## Translation

Thirty spokes meet in a single hub.
Through the emptiness at its center, the cart can roll.

Through the emptiness it contains,
A clay vessel becomes useful.

Through the empty spaces cut into walls,
A room becomes useful.

Therefore, filled space creates potential.
Empty space makes things useful.
