# Chapter 76

## Translation

*(untranslated)*

## Source

### Block 1

| 原文 | Pinyin | Literal |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 人之生也柔弱，其死也堅強。 | Rén zhī shēng yě róu ruò, qí sǐ yě jiān qiáng. | Human life is supple/soft and weak; at death it is rigid and hard/strong. |
| 萬物草木之生也柔脆，其死也枯槁。 | Wàn wù cǎo mù zhī shēng yě róu cuì, qí sǐ yě kū gǎo. | The ten thousand things—grass and trees—are in life supple and fragile; at death they are withered and dried. |

### Block 2

| 原文 | Pinyin | Literal |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 故堅強者死之徒， | Gù jiān qiáng zhě sǐ zhī tú, | Therefore the rigid and hard/strong are the disciples/company of death, |
| 柔弱者生之徒。 | Róu ruò zhě shēng zhī tú. | The supple and weak are the disciples/company of life. |

### Block 3

| 原文 | Pinyin | Literal |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 是以兵強則不勝，木強則折。 | Shì yǐ bīng qiáng zé bù shèng, mù qiáng zé zhé. | Therefore, if troops are hard/strong, they will not win; if a tree is hard/strong, it will snap. |
| 強大處下，柔弱處上。 | Qiáng dà chǔ xià, róu ruò chǔ shàng. | The hard/strong and big reside below, the supple and weak reside above. |
