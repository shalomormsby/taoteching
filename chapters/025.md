# Chapter 25

## Translation

Something formed in the undifferentiated, born before sky and earth.  
Silent. Empty. Solitary. Unchanging.  
It moves in a circle, never in danger,  
and can be taken as the mother of the world.

Its true name, I will never know. Forcing a label upon it, I call it the Tao.  
Forcing a description for it, I call it the vast.

The vast means expanding, expanding means going far, going far means returning.

Therefore, the Tao is vast, the sky is vast, the earth is vast, and humans are also vast.  
Within all bounds there are four vast things, and one of them is human.

Humans model themselves on the earth,  
Earth models itself on the sky,  
The sky models itself on the Tao,  
The Tao models itself on being what it is.

## Notes

**On 人 in the four greats — an editorial call, not a manuscript one.** Our base text reads 王 (*wáng* — king) and we render **human**. Every excavated witness reads 王, Guodian (~300 BCE) included; the human reading is transmitted, from 傅奕 (Tang) and 范應元 (Song), and rests on the seam in the received text itself — the next line is 人法地, *humans follow earth*. Corrected 2026-08-20 from an earlier note that had the manuscript evidence backwards. Apparatus: `notes/manuscript.md` · Ch 25.

**周行而不殆 → "It moves in a circle, never in danger" — a dropped clause restored and a borrowed word returned.** *(2026-08-31, Pass C, `WORKLIST.md` T2-11.)* *(was: "Inexhaustible, it can be considered the mother of the world.")* Two faults in one line. **周行 (*zhōu xíng* — "moves in a circle") was not translated at all**, a debt `WORKLIST` already carried. And 不殆 was given *inexhaustible*, which belongs to 窮 (*qióng*) at ch 6 and ch 35.

**殆 (*dài*) is peril, not exhaustion.** 說文解字: 危也. The graph is 歺 (*è*), the bare-bone radical — the death element in 死 (*sǐ* — to die) and 殃 (*yāng* — calamity). 王弼 (*Wáng Bì*) reads the line 周行無所不至而**免殆** — *"it circulates and reaches everywhere, yet **escapes peril**"* — with the Siku compilers' collation note 〔案免永樂大典作危〕, *"for 免, the Yongle Dadian reads 危."* **Peril under either reading; exhaustion under neither.**

**The circle matters to the chapter.** 周行 is the motion the next movement names — 大曰逝，逝曰遠，遠曰反 (*"the vast means going, going means far, far means returning"*) — so dropping it left the return with nothing to return along.
