# Chapter 39

## Translation

From the very beginning, these things draw from the One:  
The cosmos draws from the One and becomes transparent.  
Earth draws from the One and becomes solid.  
Spirit draws from the One and becomes vital.  
Valleys draw from the One and become lush.  
All things draw from the One and come into being.  
Wise rulers draw from the One and become true anchors for the world.

These are the natural consequences:  
If the cosmos lacked transparency, it would be obliterated.  
If the earth lacked solidity, it would disintegrate.  
If spirit lacked vitality, it would fade away.  
If valleys lacked lushness, they would dry up.  
If all things lacked being, they would cease to exist.  
If wise rulers lost their anchoring, they would fall.

Therefore, the noble is rooted in the humble.  
The high is grounded in the low.  
This is why wise rulers call themselves orphaned, meager, and unworthy.  
Isn’t this recognizing the lowly as the root? Isn’t it?

Therefore, the highest praise is to have no praise.  
Don’t strive to chime and shine like polished jade.  
Instead, clatter and remain rough like common stone.

| **地得一以寧；** | Dì dé yī yǐ níng | Earth obtained the One, and by it became tranquil/solid/stable. |
| **神得一以靈；** | Shén dé yī yǐ líng | The spirits (invisible forces) obtained the One, and by it became numinous/active/alive. |
| **谷得一以盈；** | Gǔ dé yī yǐ yíng | The valleys obtained the One, and by it became full/replenished. |
| **萬物得一以生；** | Wàn wù dé yī yǐ shēng | The ten thousand things obtained the One, and by it came to life/were born. |
| **侯王得一以為天下貞。** | Hóu wáng dé yī yǐ wéi tiān xià zhēn | Sovereigns and kings obtained the One, and by it became the true standard/anchor for the world. |
