# Chapter 31

## Translation

Understand that even the most finely-crafted weapons truly are instruments of ill omen.  
All creatures generally despise them.  
Therefore, one who is aligned with the Tao does not dwell with them.

In times of peace, the sage chooses creation. When the military must be used, they choose destruction.  
Weapons are tools of ill omen. They are not the tools of the sage.  
When the circumstances are unavoidable, calm detachment is essential.

Achieve victory, but don’t revel in it.  
To glorify battle is to rejoice in killing people.  
Understand that whomever rejoices in killing people cannot achieve their purpose in the world.

Auspicious occasions honor the left; inauspicious occasions honor the right.  
The lieutenant general stands on the left, the supreme commander on the right,  
which is to say they are placed by the rites of mourning.  
When many have been killed, weep for them with sorrow and grief.  
A victory in battle should be treated like a funeral rite.
