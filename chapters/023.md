# Chapter 23

## Translation

Speak sparingly, for things go of themselves.  
Therefore a squall doesn’t last all morning. Torrential rain doesn’t last all day.  
Who acts this way? Sky and earth.  
If sky and earth can’t sustain intensity for long, how much less can we?

Therefore, the one who aligns their actions with the Tao merges with the Tao,  
Whoever acts with profound integrity merges with profound integrity,  
Whoever loses merges with the loss.

Those merged with the Tao, the Tao gladly receives.  
Those merged with profound integrity, profound integrity gladly receives.  
Those merged with loss, loss gladly receives.  
Where trust is withheld, trust is withheld in return.

## Notes

**信不足焉，有不信焉 is rendered differently here than in Ch 17, deliberately.** Ch 17 is a chapter about rulers and supplies *leaders / the people* as the subjects the Chinese omits; Ch 23 is about 同 (*tóng* — merging) and would be narrowed by that frame, so it keeps the subject unsupplied. Flagged for reconsideration in the editing pass. Full reasoning in `notes/translation.md`.
