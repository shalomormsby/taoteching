# Chapter 77

*(untranslated)*

## Source

### Block 1

| 原文 | Pinyin | Literal |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 天之道，其猶張弓與？ | Tiān zhī dào, qí yóu zhāng gōng yǔ? | The Tao of Heaven, is it not like drawing a bow? |
| 高者抑之，下者舉之； | Gāo zhě yì zhī, xià zhě jǔ zhī; | The high is lowered, the low is raised; |
| 有餘者損之，不足者補之。 | Yǒu yú zhě sǔn zhī, bù zú zhě bǔ zhī. | Those who have excess, it diminishes; those who are insufficient, it supplements. |

### Block 2

| 原文 | Pinyin | Literal |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 天之道，損有餘而補不足。 | Tiān zhī dào, sǔn yǒu yú ér bǔ bù zú. | The Tao of Heaven: diminishes those who have excess and supplements those who are insufficient. |
| 人之道，則不然，損不足以奉有餘。 | Rén zhī dào, zé bù rán, sǔn bù zú yǐ fèng yǒu yú. | The Tao of humanity is not like this: it diminishes those who are insufficient to serve those who have excess. |

### Block 3

| 原文 | Pinyin | Literal |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 孰能有餘以奉天下？唯有道者。 | Shú néng yǒu yú yǐ fèng tiān xià? Wéi yǒu dào zhě. | Who can have excess to serve the world? Only the one who has the Tao. |
| 是以聖人為而不恃，功成而不處，其不欲見賢。 | Shì yǐ shèng rén wéi ér bù shì, gōng chéng ér bù chù, qí bù yù jiàn xián. | Therefore the Sage acts but does not rely, accomplishes work but does not take credit; they do not wish to display their worth. |
