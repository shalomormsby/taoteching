# Chapter 36

## Translation

Contraction requires firm expansion.  
Weakening requires firm strengthening.  
Abolishing requires firm elevation.  
Removing requires firm giving.

This is called subtle illumination.  
Soft and weak overcome hard and strong.

Fish cannot detach from the deep abyss.  
The tools of nature cannot be shown to humans.
