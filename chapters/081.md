## Source

### Block 1

| 原文 | Pinyin | Literal |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 信言不美，美言不信。 | Xìn yán bù měi, měi yán bù xìn. | Truthful words are not beautiful/decorative; beautiful/decorative words are not truthful. |
| 善者不辯，辯者不善。 | Shàn zhě bù biàn, biàn zhě bù shàn. | Those who are "good" (authentic) do not argue/debate; those who argue/debate are not "good." |
| 知者不博，博者不知。 | Zhī zhě bù bó, bó zhě bù zhī. | Those who truly know are not broadly learned (in superficial knowledge); those who are broadly learned do not truly know. |
