# Chapter 41

## Translation

The highest student hears the Tao and practices it with diligence.
The average student hears the Tao and keeps some, forgets some.
The lowest student hears the Tao and laughs aloud at it.
If it weren’t laughed at, the Tao wouldn’t be what it is.

Therefore, it is said:
Bright Tao seems dark, advancing Tao seems to retreat, smooth Tao seems rough.
The highest integrity seems like a valley, and true purity seems soiled.
Vast integrity seems insufficient, well-founded integrity seems slack, and the most solid truth seems to waver.

The great square has no corners, the great vessel is never completed,
The great sound can barely be heard, the great image has no form.

The Tao is hidden and nameless.
Yet the Tao alone masterfully provides for all things and brings them to completion.

**Notes:**

1. "ctext reads 晚成 'late-completed'; we follow the Mawangdui/Guodian reading 免/曼成 'never completed.'"
