### Block 1

| 民之飢，以其上食稅之多，是以飢。 | Mín zhī jī, yǐ qí shàng shí shuì zhī duō, shì yǐ jī. | The hunger of the people, because their superiors consume taxes in such quantity, therefore they are hungry. |

### Block 2

| 原文 | Pinyin | Literal |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 民之難治，以其上之有為，是以難治。 | Mín zhī nán zhì, yǐ qí shàng zhī yǒu wéi, shì yǐ nán zhì. | The people are difficult to govern, because their superiors have "doing" (interference), therefore they are difficult to govern. |

| 夫唯無以生為者，是賢於貴生。 | Fú wéi wú yǐ shēng wéi zhě, shì xián yú guì shēng. | Only those who do not use "living" as a "doing," are worthier than those who value/prize life. |
