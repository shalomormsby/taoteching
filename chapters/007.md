# Chapter 7

## Translation

The cosmos is vast. The earth is everlasting.
Vast and everlasting,
Sky and soil don’t struggle to survive.
They endure without limit.

Placing themselves last, the sage is first;
Unconcerned for themselves, their spirits endure.

Free of selfish desires,
The sage simply does what needs to be done.
