# Chapter 22

## Translation

Yield and remain unbroken. Be wronged to be straightened,  
Be hollowed-out to be filled. Be worn out to be renewed.  
Hold little to gain much. Have much, become confused.

Therefore, the Sage embraces the One and sets the example for everyone.

The Sage does not display self, and is therefore luminous,  
Does not debate, therefore is unrivaled,  
Does not boast, therefore is preeminent,  
Doesn’t take pride in anything, therefore is eternal.

Because they don’t compete, no one in the cosmos can compete with them.  
When the ancients say, “Yield and remain unbroken,” were these empty words?  
Try and see for yourself.
