# Chapter 62

## Translation

*(untranslated)*

## Source

### Block 1

| 原文 | Pinyin | Literal |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 道者萬物之奧。 | Dào zhě wàn wù zhī ào. | The Tao is the profound mystery/sanctuary of the ten thousand things. |
| 善人之寶，不善人之所保。 | Shàn rén zhī bǎo, bù shàn rén zhī suǒ bǎo. | The treasure of the masterful person, the refuge/protection of the un-masterful person. |

### Block 2

| 原文 | Pinyin | Literal |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 美言可以市尊，美行可以加人。 | Měi yán kě yǐ shì zūn, měi xíng kě yǐ jiā rén. | Beautiful words can buy honor/respect, beautiful actions can elevate one above others. |
| 人之不善，何棄之有？ | Rén zhī bù shàn, hé qì zhī yǒu? | If a person is un-masterful, why abandon/discard them? |

### Block 3

| 原文 | Pinyin | Literal |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 故立天子，置三公， | Gù lì tiān zǐ, zhì sān gōng, | Therefore, when establishing the Son of Heaven, or appointing the Three Dukes, |
| 雖有拱璧以先駟馬， | Suī yǒu gǒng bì yǐ xiān sì mǎ, | Even though one has large jade disks preceded by a team of four horses, |
| 不如坐進此道。 | Bù rú zuò jìn cǐ dào. | It is not as good as sitting and advancing/offering this Tao. |

### Block 4

| 原文 | Pinyin | Literal |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 古之所以貴此道者何？ | Gǔ zhī suǒ yǐ guì cǐ dào zhě hé? | Why did the ancients value this Tao so highly? |
| 不曰：求以得，有罪以免邪？ | Bù yuē: qiú yǐ dé, yǒu zuì yǐ miǎn xié? | Did they not say: Seeking, one obtains it; having faults/sins, one is pardoned/freed? |
| 故為天下貴。 | Gù wéi tiān xià guì. | Therefore it is the most valued in the world. |
