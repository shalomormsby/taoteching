# Chapter 71

## Translation

*(untranslated)*

## Source

| 原文 | Pinyin | Literal |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 知不知，上； | Zhī bù zhī, shàng; | Knowing that one does not know, is superior/highest; |
| 不知知，病。 | Bù zhī zhī, bìng. | Not knowing yet thinking one knows, is a disease/flaw. |
| 夫唯病病，是以不病。 | Fú wéi bìng bìng, shì yǐ bù bìng. | Only by recognizing the disease as a disease, therefore one is not diseased. |
| 聖人不病，以其病病，是以不病。 | Shèng rén bù bìng, yǐ qí bìng bìng, shì yǐ bù bìng. | The Sage is not diseased, because they recognize the disease as a disease, therefore they are not diseased. |
