# Chapter 29

## Translation

Those who wish to force their will upon the world — I see that this cannot be done.  
The world is a sacred system. It cannot be forced or controlled.  
Those who try to force it ruin it. Those who try to grasp it lose it.

Therefore, sometimes leading, sometimes following,  
Sometimes breathing heavily, sometimes breathing easily,  
Sometimes strong, sometimes weak,  
Sometimes safe, sometimes imperiled.

Therefore the sage removes the extreme, the extravagant, the excessive.
