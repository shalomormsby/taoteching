# Chapter 67

## Translation

Everyone says my Tao is great, and looks like nothing.  
Only because it is great does it look like nothing.  
If it looked like anything, it would long since have been slight.

I have three treasures. I hold them and keep them safe.  
The first is tenderness.  
The second is frugality.  
The third is never pushing to be first in the world.

Tenderness, and so you can be brave.  
Frugality, and so you can reach far.  
Never pushing to be first in the world, and so you can become chief of the vessels.

Now: to abandon tenderness and grab at courage,  
to abandon frugality and grab at reach,  
to abandon the rear and grab at the front.  
That is death.

Tenderness. Fight with it and you win, defend with it and you hold firm.  
When nature would rescue someone, it guards them with tenderness.

## Notes

**天下皆謂我道大 → "Everyone says my Tao is great."** *(2026-09-06, Shalom's call. Was: "Everyone says I am vast, and look like nothing.")* **道 (*dào*) had been dropped and the whole passage handed to the speaker.** The Chinese says 我**道**大 — *my Tao is great* — and the three lines that follow are about the Tao, not about the person speaking. With 道 restored the subject runs *it* throughout, and 王弼's argument lands: 肖則失其所以為大矣 — *"to resemble [things] is to lose what makes it great."* The Tao looks like nothing because anything it resembled would have shrunk it to that thing's size.

**大 → "great"**, completing the sweep begun at ch 25 — 大 is *great* in 41 lines and was *vast* only here and at ch 25.

**細 (*xì*) → "slight," matching ch 63.** *(Was "petty.")* 細 occurs in two chapters and had two Englishes; ch 63's 為大於其細 and 天下大事，必作於細 already read **slight** twice. *Petty* also imported a moral verdict the character does not carry — 細 is the **silk radical** 糸, thread-fine, a matter of size.

**Not touched: 夫唯 (*fū wéi*) still reads "Only because" here.** It is a formula across **11 chapters and 12 lines** and now carries at least three Englishes book-wide — *"Only because"* (67, 70), *"Precisely because"* (15), and a bare *"Because"* (2). Settling it is a term pass, not a line fix. `WORKLIST.md` T2-43.
