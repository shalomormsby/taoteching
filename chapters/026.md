# Chapter 26

## Translation

The heavy is the root of the ungrounded.  
Silence is the ruler of the agitated.

Therefore, the sage travels all day without becoming disconnected from sustenance.  
Even when the sights are glorious, their equanimity is undisturbed.

How could a great ruler become ungrounded in their being?  
To become ungrounded is to lose connection with one’s roots.  
To become agitated is to lose one’s mastery.
