## Source

### Block 1

| 原文 | Pinyin | Literal |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 民不畏威，則大威至。 | Mín bù wèi wēi, zé dà wēi zhì. | When the people do not fear authority/might, then great authority/might arrives. |
| 無狎其所居，無厭其所生。 | Wú xiá qí suǒ jū, wú yàn qí suǒ shēng. | Do not crowd/harass their place of residence, do not oppress/weary their livelihood. |
| 夫唯不厭，是以不厭。 | Fú wéi bù yàn, shì yǐ bù yàn. | Precisely because one does not oppress them, therefore they do not find it oppressive. |

### Block 2

| 原文 | Pinyin | Literal |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 是以聖人自知，不自見； | Shì yǐ shèng rén zì zhī, bù zì jiàn; | Therefore the Sage knows self, but does not display self; |
| 自愛，不自貴。 | Zì ài, bù zì guì. | Loves self, but does not esteem/exalt self. |
| 故去彼取此。 | Gù qù bǐ qǔ cǐ. | Therefore discard that and choose this. |
