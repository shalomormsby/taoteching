# Chapter 8

## Translation

Supreme goodness is like water.  
Without contending, it nourishes the countless things.  
It dwells where the crowd will not go.  
And is therefore one with the Tao.

In a dwelling, it is a solid foundation.  
In heart, it is limitless depth.  
In the act of giving, it is pure kindness.  
In speech, it is trustworthy.  
In governing, it is good order.  
In daily affairs, it is skillful.  
In action, it has good timing.

Because it doesn’t contend,  
It is always blameless.
