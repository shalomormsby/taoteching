# Chapter 33

## Translation

One who knows others is intelligent.  
One who knows themselves sees clearly.

One who overcomes others has aggressive power.  
One who overcomes themselves has true strength.

One who knows they have enough is truly wealthy.  
One who acts with perseverance has willpower.

One who does not lose their rootedness, endures.  
One who dies but does not perish has true longevity.
