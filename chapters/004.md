# Chapter 4

## Translation

The Tao is a vessel that never runs empty.  
It is the source of the ten-thousand things.

It blunts sharpness,  
Untangles the tangled,  
Softens brilliance,  
Is one with every grain of dust.

Unfathomably deep, it seems to exist,  
Although its source is a mystery that’s older than god.
