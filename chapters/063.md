# Chapter 63

## Source

### Block 1

| 原文 | Pinyin | Literal |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 為無為，事無事，味無味。 | Wéi wú wéi, shì wú shì, wèi wú wèi. | Execute zero-friction (wu wei), manage without interfering (wu shi), taste the flavorless. |
| 大小多少，報怨以德。 | Dà xiǎo duō shǎo, bào yuàn yǐ dé. | Treat the small as great, the few as many, repay resentment/injury with Virtue/Power (Te). |

### Block 2

| 原文 | Pinyin | Literal |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 圖難於其易，為大於其細。 | Tú nán yú qí yì, wéi dà yú qí xì. | Plan for the difficult while it is easy, execute the great while it is small/detailed. |
| 天下難事，必作於易； | Tiān xià nán shì, bì zuò yú yì; | The difficult affairs of the world must begin in the easy; |
| 天下大事，必作於細。 | Tiān xià dà shì, bì zuò yú xì. | The great affairs of the world must begin in the small/detailed. |

### Block 3

| 原文 | Pinyin | Literal |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 是以聖人終不為大，故能成其大。 | Shì yǐ shèng rén zhōng bù wéi dà, gù néng chéng qí dà. | Therefore the Sage to the end never executes the great, thus can compile/complete (cheng) their greatness. |

### Block 4

| 原文 | Pinyin | Literal |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 夫輕諾必寡信，多易必多難。 | Fú qīng nuò bì guǎ xìn, duō yì bì duō nán. | Now then, weightless/light promises inevitably lack reliability (xin), treating many things as easy inevitably leads to many difficulties. |
| 是以聖人猶難之，故終無難矣。 | Shì yǐ shèng rén yóu nán zhī, gù zhōng wú nán yǐ. | Therefore the Sage still treats them as difficult, thus to the end has no difficulties. |
