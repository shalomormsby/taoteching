# Chapter 28

## Translation

To know assertive energy, we must attune to yielding energy,  
Embody the deep ravine of the world.  
Embodying the deep ravine of the world — this is where integrity accumulates without leaving.  
Become like an infant again.

To know light, we must attune to darkness.  
Embody the pattern of the world.  
Embodying the pattern of the world — this is how integrity manifests without deviation.  
Return to the Limitless.

To know glory, we must welcome disgrace.  
Embody the reservoir of the world.  
Embodying the reservoir of the world, integrity is abundant.  
Return to the Uncarved Block.

When the Uncarved Block is carved, it is made into various tools.  
The sage uses them as the master orchestrator.  
Therefore, the Great Orchestrator itself does not carve.
