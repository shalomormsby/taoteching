# Chapter 21

## Translation

The greatest integrity simply follows the Tao.  
In the manifest realm, the Tao appears intangible and dark.  
Within its intangible darkness appear forms.  
Within these intangible, dark forms appear entities.

Within its dark depths lies the primordial mass of creation.  
This primordial mass is profoundly real and infallibly reliable.

From the beginning of time to this present moment,  
It serves as the unerasable source code of all creation.  
How do I know this is the source code of all creation? By this, now.
