# Chapter 48

## Translation

In pursuit of learning, you increase daily;
in pursuit of the Tao, you decrease daily.
Decreasing and decreasing, until you reach non-doing.
Non-doing, yet nothing is left undone.

The world only comes to you by not interfering;
the moment you grab at it, it slips away.

always won by non-interference.
Once you resort to meddling, you cannot win the world.
