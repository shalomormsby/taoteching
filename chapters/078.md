# Chapter 78

## Source

### Block 1

| 原文 | Pinyin | Literal |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 天下莫柔弱於水， | Tiān xià mò róu ruò yú shuǐ, | In the world, nothing is more supple and weak than water, |
| 而攻堅強者莫之能勝，以其無以易之。 | Ér gōng jiān qiáng zhě mò zhī néng shèng, yǐ qí wú yǐ yì zhī. | Yet in attacking the hard and strong, nothing can surpass it, because nothing can replace it. |

### Block 2

| 原文 | Pinyin | Literal |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 弱之勝強，柔之勝剛，天下莫不知，莫能行。 | Ruò zhī shèng qiáng, róu zhī shèng gāng, tiān xià mò bù zhī, mò néng xíng. | That the weak conquers the strong, the supple conquers the hard/rigid—the world knows this, but no one can practice it. |

### Block 3

| 原文 | Pinyin | Literal |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 是以聖人云：受國之垢，是謂社稷主；受國之不祥，是謂天下王。 | Shì yǐ shèng rén yún: shòu guó zhī gòu, shì wèi shè jì zhǔ; shòu guó zhī bù xiáng, shì wèi tiān xià wáng. | Therefore the Sage says: To accept the state's filth is to be the Lord of the Altars (the people); to accept the state's misfortune is to be the King of the World. |
| 正言若反。 | Zhèng yán ruò fǎn. | Right words seem like the opposite (paradoxical). |
