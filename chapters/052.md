# Chapter 52

## Translation

The world has an origin, which serves as the mother of the world.
Find our mother, and you know her children.
Know her children, return and hold fast to our mother,
and to the end of your days, no harm will come to you.

Block the openings, close the doors, and all your life will be struggle-free.
Open the openings, multiply your affairs, and your life is beyond saving.

To recognize things in their smallest form is clear-seeing.
To stay with the yielding is strength.

Use the outer light, return to your inner clarity,
and you bring no calamity upon yourself.

This is the practice of the ever-present.
