# Chapter 24

## Translation

One who stands on tiptoes doesn’t stand firmly.  
One who takes the longest strides can’t keep walking for long.

One who shows off is not clear.  
One who insists on being right is not prominent.  
One who boasts has no accomplishment.  
One who is full of pride does not endure.

From the perspective of the Tao, these are wasteful and superfluous.  
The material world naturally repels them.  
Those aligned with the Tao steer clear of them.
