# Chapter 42

## Translation

The Tao births the One, the One births the Two, the Two births the Three, the Three births all things.
All things carry yin on their backs and embrace yang with their arms, blending the vital breath into harmony.
that
What most people hate is to be called orphaned, meager, or unworthy,
Yet kings and lords take these very descriptions for themselves.

For so it is: what is decreased is thereby increased, and what is increased is thereby decreased.

What others teach, I also teach:
The violent and unyielding do not die a natural death.
I take this as the root of learning.
