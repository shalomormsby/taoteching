# Chapter 17

## Translation

About the greatest leaders, little is known.
Good leaders are widely loved and praised.
Poor leaders are feared.
The worst leaders are despised by all.

When leaders don’t trust the people, no one trusts the leaders.

The greatest leaders speak sparingly.
When their work is done,
The people all say, “We did this by ourselves.”
