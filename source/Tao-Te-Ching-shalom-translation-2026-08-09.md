## Chapter 1

The Tao that can be spoken is not the ever-present Tao.  
The name that can be named is not the ever-present name.  
The nameless is the beginning of sky and earth.  
The named is the mother of all things.

Ever desireless, observe its subtlety;  
Ever desiring, observe its boundaries.

These two have the same origin, but differ in name.  
Together, they are called profound darkness.

Dark, and dark again, the gate of all subtleties.

## Chapter 2

When the world knows beauty as beauty, ugliness arises.   
When the world knows good as good, the bad arises.

Therefore being and non-being give birth to each other,   
Difficult and easy complete each other,   
Long and short measure each other,   
High and low depend on each other,   
Sound and tone harmonize each other,   
Front and back follow each other.

Therefore, the sage acts without effort and teaches without words.  
Myriad things arise and none are turned away.   
They birth without possessing and act without presuming. 

With no concern for credit, the sage’s work endures eternally.

## Chapter 3

By not exalting the exceptional, the people have no cause to compete.  
By not valuing the precious, no one becomes a thief.  
By not displaying the desirable, the people are not perturbed.

Therefore the sage  
Empties minds and fills bellies  
Weakens striving and strengthens the bones of the people.

Without creating desire or cunning,  
Without causing the cunning to act,  
Without effort, the sage leaves nothing out of order.

It is the source of the ten-thousand things.

It blunts sharpness,  
Untangles the tangled,

## Chapter 5

The sky and earth lack benevolence and treat everything like straw dogs.  
The sage lacks benevolence and treats everyone like straw dogs.

Isn't the space between sky and earth like a bellows?  
Empty but never depleted, the more it moves, the more it yields.

Speaking of this is a dead end.  
Instead, hold the center in silence.

## Chapter 8

Without competing, it nourishes all things.   
It seeks the lowest places.   
And is therefore one with the Tao. 

In a dwelling, it is a solid foundation.   
In the heart-mind, it is limitless depth.   
In the act of giving,  it is pure kindness.   
In spoken words, it is sincerity.   
In governing, it is good order.   
In daily affairs, it is skillful.   
In action, it has good timing. 

Because it doesn’t compete,   
It is always blameless.

Birthing and nourishing all things,   
Giving birth without possessing,  
Acting without presuming,

## Chapter 13

Both favor and disgrace cause alarm.   
Value misfortune as your own body. 

What does it mean that favor and disgrace both cause alarm?   
You must stoop to receive favor.   
Receiving it causes alarm.   
Losing it also causes alarm.   
This is why favor and disgrace both cause alarm. 

What does it mean to value misfortune as your own body?   
I only experience misfortune because I have a body.   
When the separate self dissolves, what troubles could I have?   
Therefore, one who values the world as their body can be entrusted with the world.  
One who loves the whole world as their own body can truly care for it.
